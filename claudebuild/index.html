03 / THE STEP UP

Level 1 taught you the tool.
Level 2 hands you the keys.

Same trainer, same tools, completely different session. One is learning. The other is making.

Level 1 — Agentic AI
Andrew leads. Everyone follows the same path.

A structured session with a defined outcome — you leave understanding what Claude can do and having built a working example alongside the room. Everyone ends up in roughly the same place, which is exactly right for a first class.

Level 2 — Build
You lead. Andrew moves between you.

Free flow. Twenty people building twenty different things, with Andrew going desk to desk — unsticking, extending, showing you the bit you didn't know to ask about. Nobody ends up in the same place, because nobody started with the same idea.

04 / HOW IT RUNS

There's no syllabus.
That's deliberate.

A fixed curriculum works when everyone wants the same thing. In Level 2 they don't — one person is adding payments, another is stuck on a database, a third wants to know why their tracking shows nothing. So the session bends to the room.

What stays fixed is the shape: you arrive with a project, you leave with it further along than you could have got alone in a week.

08 / QUESTIONS

Answered.

01
Do I really need Level 1 first?
Yes. It's the one hard rule on this course. Level 2 opens on your own project with no recap — without Level 1 you'd spend the session catching up. Level 1 runs online and in person, including the same morning as every Build date.
02
Why is there no online version?
Because this class is Andrew moving between twenty desks for three and a half hours, looking at what's actually on your screen. That doesn't survive a Zoom grid. If you want the online option, that's Level 1.
03
What if I don't know what to build?
Come anyway. Most people arrive with a rough idea rather than a plan, and part of the session is turning "I've been meaning to…" into something running. If you're genuinely blank, Andrew will hand you a project worth building.
04
Do I need to be able to code?
No. That's the entire premise — Claude writes the code, you direct it. You'll see Claude Code, databases and deployment, but you're the one deciding what gets built, not typing it out.
05
What do I need to bring?
Your own laptop and a Claude Pro subscription — you'll work in your own account all session so everything you build goes home with you. Nothing else.
06
How much is it, and what do I get back?
S$240 for the 3.5-hour session, capped at 20 people, with follow-up support after class. You also earn 10% back in points towards your next class — 1 point = S$1, applied automatically. Full terms are in the Student Portal.
07
What if the date doesn't fill up?
In-person classes need a minimum number of participants before they can go ahead. If a date doesn't reach it, we may ask you to move to another date — everything you've paid carries over, and we'll tell you early enough to plan around it.
