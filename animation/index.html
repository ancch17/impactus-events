    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Do I need any video editing or design experience?
    No. The masterclass is built for beginners — you'll learn every tool hands-on from scratch, step by step.
    What do I need before the session?
    A Google AI Pro subscription (from US$19.99/mo, cancel anytime) for access to Google Flow, plus free accounts for Suno and CapCut, and Zoom to join the live class.
    Is the class live or pre-recorded?
    Always live and interactive with Dr. Andrew Chew — never a recording. You can sit it online on Zoom, or face-to-face in Singapore. Various dates are available throughout the year.
    What's the difference between the online and face-to-face class?
    The teaching, the tools and what you walk away with are identical. Face-to-face you get an extra 30 minutes (3.5 hours instead of 3) and Andrew beside you when a shot won't behave. Online you keep your own setup and skip the travel. Online is S$180, face-to-face is S$240.
    What if the face-to-face date doesn't fill up?
    Face-to-face classes need a minimum number of participants before they can go ahead — we won't run a room for two people. If a date doesn't reach it, we may ask you to move to another date, and everything you've paid carries over to the new one. We'll always tell you early enough to plan around it.
    Where is the face-to-face class held?
    Paya Lebar Square, Singapore — right on top of the MRT interchange. The exact unit and directions are sent to you after you book.
    How big is the class?
    It's a hands-on session, so the class deliberately stays small enough that you can ask a question and actually get it answered.
    Should I do this class or the Automation one?
    Start here. This Level 1 class teaches you to direct each shot yourself, so you understand how the craft works. Level 2 — AI Animation Automation hands the pipeline to Claude and you supervise it, which is what you want once you're producing at volume. There's a full side-by-side comparison further up this page.
    Will I actually create a video?
    Yes. The session is hands-on — you'll generate video with Flow, music with Suno, and edit it together in CapCut during the three hours.
    How much is it and how do I register?
    Online is S$180 for three hours live; face-to-face is S$240 for three and a half. Both are 100% hands-on with Dr. Andrew Chew, with follow-up query support after class, and both earn you 10% back in points towards your next class (1 point = S$1). All dates are in the Reserve Seat portal — booking opens shortly and you'll receive your joining details by email.
