How the count works

Each person is counted once, matched across your WhatsApp class groups and communities, the booking system and the SUSS class lists by mobile, booking account and SUSS student ID. SUSS learners who took more than one module count once. Where only a headcount exists (invoices, class lists), the extra learners are counted without names. Classes where you were a TA are left out of the learner count. Partner class lists are never stored, and SUSS learners are kept as anonymous IDs only.
