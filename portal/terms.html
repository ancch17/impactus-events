Terms & Conditions

Impactus Learning Pte Ltd · Last updated 26 July 2026

1. Prices and payment

All prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD). Your place in a class is confirmed when payment is received — we accept PayNow, card and GrabPay. A seat is not reserved until payment clears.

2. Minimum class size for in-person classes

In-person (F2F) classes run with a minimum of 5 participants. If a date does not reach the minimum, we will contact you and offer you a place on another date at no extra cost. Anything you paid, and any points you used, carry over to the new date.

If none of the dates we offer works for you, write to us at andrew@impactuslearning.com and we will sort it out with you individually.

Online classes have no minimum class size.

3. If we cancel or move a class

If we cancel or move a class for any other reason, the same applies — we will offer you a place on another date, carrying over what you paid, and we will tell you as early as we can. If that doesn't work for you, get in touch and we will sort it out with you.

4. If you need to change your date

Life happens. If you can't make your class, contact us at andrew@impactuslearning.com as early as you can and we will do our best to move you to a later date. Transfers are subject to seats being available on the date you want.

Course fees are otherwise non-refundable. If something serious has happened, write in and tell us — we consider these individually, on their merits.

5. Points

  • You earn points worth 10% of the cash you actually pay for a class. 1 point = S$1.
  • Points are credited once your payment is confirmed.
  • Points are applied automatically to your next booking — your whole available balance comes off the amount due, up to the full price of that class.
  • Points do not expire for as long as Impactus Learning is operating.
  • Points are personal to you. They are not transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Points are earned on cash paid, not on the list price. A class paid for entirely with points, a free pass or a 100% discount code earns no new points.
  • We may change the earning rate in future with notice. Points you have already earned will always be honoured.

6. Discount codes and free passes

Discount codes and free passes are single-use per student unless stated otherwise, cannot be combined with other codes, and have no cash value.

7. Class materials

Slides, recordings and materials are provided for your personal learning. Please don't redistribute or resell them.
